I · Couture
The Frescoes
A palazzo where the ceiling competes with the gown
Painted flowers above, embroidered ones below. Only one may win.
    
Why it works  In a palazzo the ceiling competes with the gown, and the gown must win. The room stays warm and low, the chandeliers fall away, the brightest light goes to the embroidery. Every flower reads before the ceiling does, on a showroom wall and on a phone.

II · Editorial
The Window
One gesture the height of the room
One gesture, the height of the room.
    
Why it works  An editorial gives a collection its attitude. Shot low in a tall window, the black silhouette takes the height of the palazzo, and the daylight behind her cuts the shape clean against gold walls. One strong line is what an editor remembers from forty pages.

III · Bridal
The Rotunda
The room turned into the frame of the train
The room becomes the frame of the train.
    
Why it works  Bridal is sold on the length of one moment. We step back and let the rotunda work: dark floor, tall windows, the train laid in a full circle at her feet. Window light keeps the white pure. Symmetry gives the gown the weight of a ceremony.
