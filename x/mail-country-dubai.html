I · Couture
The Mosaic
Shot on the mosaic rather than in front of it
The myth is underfoot. The gown answers it.
    
Why it works  In the Gulf a gown is read as wealth of work, so the handwork needs an equal partner. Shot from above, on a floor that took years to lay, the frame becomes ornament with the dress at its centre. Warm stone keeps gold and ivory rich without one added light.

II · Editorial
The Plumes
Flowers are composed, not picked
Flowers are composed, not picked. Plume by plume.
    
Why it works  The desert leaves nowhere to hide. Hard sun and a clean sky give colour the clarity of print, and the pampas repeats the texture of the gown in nature. Handwork answering handwork. One bold colour against sand stops the eye in the first second.

III · Bridal
The Desert Train
Bridal carried into open desert
Crystal and tulle, read by the sun alone.
    
Why it works  Bridal is sold on light more than on the dress. With the sun behind her, tulle turns translucent and every crystal finds its own highlight. The dunes give the train the length it was cut for. Sand tones keep the white pure, never grey.
