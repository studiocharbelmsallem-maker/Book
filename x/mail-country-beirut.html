I · Couture
The Atelier
Red-carpet weight, shot for the moment before the flashbulb
One warm light in an old room. The satin does the rest.
    
Why it works  Couture is judged on construction, so the light must explain it. One hard source, low and to the side, draws every fold and leaves the room in shadow. Nothing in the frame is brighter than the dress. The handwork becomes the only thing the eye can read.

II · Editorial
The Coast
Winter couture walked across raw Beirut stone
Embroidery against volcanic stone. The sea keeps its distance.
    
Why it works  An editorial gives a collection a place to belong. Pale beadwork on dark rock is the strongest contrast nature offers: it reads at three metres and rewards at thirty centimetres. The cliff gives the train its scale. The open sky keeps the frame calm.

III · Casual
The Field
Customised to every campaign, aimed at the woman it was cut for
A pleated sleeve, a dry field, a wide pale sky. A life, not an occasion.
    
Why it works  Ready-to-wear sells a life, not an occasion. Out of the studio, in open daylight, pleats move with the wind and fabric shows its true weight. White, straw and sky. The silhouette leads, and the image reads in one breath on a phone.
