Charbel Msallem Privacy
Charbel Msallem Studios · Beirut

Your details, held with care.

When you write to the studio, through a form on Facebook or Instagram, by email or on WhatsApp, what you share is used for one thing: to answer you personally.

Who holds your details

Charbel Msallem Studios, the fashion photography and film studio of Charbel Msallem, based in Beirut, Lebanon. You can reach the studio at studiocharbelmsallem@gmail.com.

What the studio receives

When you fill in a studio form, the studio receives what you enter, usually:

  • your name and the name of your house
  • your email address and WhatsApp number
  • your city

Meta also tells the studio which form and which advertisement you came from, and when you sent it.

Why

To reply to your enquiry, to prepare a proposal if you ask for one, and to keep a record of the conversation. You are not added to any mailing list, and your details are never sold, rented or traded.

Where your details are kept

In the studio's own email and calendar (Google), moved there from the form by Zapier, an automation service. These services only store and carry the information on the studio's behalf. The form itself is provided by Meta, under Meta's own privacy policy.

How long

For as long as the conversation is useful to you, and no longer than two years after the last exchange. You can ask for deletion at any time before that.

Your choice

You may ask to see what the studio holds about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. Write to studiocharbelmsallem@gmail.com and it will be done within thirty days. If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, these rights are yours under the GDPR, and the studio relies on your request to be contacted as the basis for using your details.

This website

The studio's online book uses Microsoft Clarity to understand how its pages are read, such as which chapters hold attention. It does not use your form details.
